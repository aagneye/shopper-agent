            How it works

            1. You ask once. Your agent picks up to four open, verified shops near you from the area's places directory.
            2. It asks their agents. Each shop's own agent answers from the shop's live stock. You approve every shop it contacts, and the shop sees only the item.
            3. You compare and hold. Answers land on the results board with the time stock was checked. A hold is confirmed in the shop's own window.

            NearShelf follows the open agentic web specification. Its agents run in your browser, and you choose the AI provider. Read its agent manifest, its agent network and the places directory.

            Demo. Neem Chowk is a fictional neighbourhood. Its shops, licences, brands and stock are made up. NearShelf never gives medical advice. In an emergency, call 112.
